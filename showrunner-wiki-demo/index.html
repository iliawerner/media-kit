03 — Insights

What works, what doesn’t

Patterns observed across 44 long-form videos in the growth period. Each is a tendency, not a law — sample sizes are small, classification is partly subjective.

Confirmed patterns

  • Apple = backbone. 60.9K median vs 14.7K without. The single cleanest signal in the dataset.
  • Concrete artefact > abstraction. “0.5mm”, “Twenty Pixels”, “Leopard Dock”, “Door Latch” — concrete nouns create the curiosity gap.
  • Nostalgia triggers. Hidden Cost of Clean Design (501K, Winamp), Windows XP (23K), First iPhone (13K).
  • Negative framing on Apple. “Admits Defeat”, “Death of Premium”, “Stopped Listening” — critique of Apple consistently pulls.
  • Title testing works. #107 grew from 4.6K → 11.8K (+158%) after a title swap nine days post-publish.
  • Single-portrait beats triptych. Confirmed on #108 and #109 thumbnail A/B tests.

What doesn’t

  • Videos over 15 minutes. Median retention 30.7%, median views 23K. The format has a clear ceiling at 900s.
  • Videos without a clear niche. “Straighten The Cursor” (23K), “Why Does It Look So Good” (7K) — outside the Apple/design-systems orbit, no boost from the existing audience.
  • UI-element + History genre. 4 of 4 failed: “Why Links Are Blue”, “Door Latch”, “Like Button is 200K Years Old”, “Interface We Lost for 2000 Years”.
  • Concept as the main object. “Reality Distortion”, “Aesthetics”, “Post-PC” — abstractions without a physical anchor underperform 4× out of 5.
  • Object thumbnails. Scroll, car, eyes, charts-as-objects — 7 of 8 failed. The harshest negative signal in the dataset.

The current problem

CTR median (4.96%) sits in the lower half of YouTube’s “strong” band. Five of the last six long-form releases were 🟡 or 🔴 against the new thresholds. Format consistency has slipped. Priorities: apply the packaging framework systematically, return to a stable release cadence, lift CTR toward 5.5–7% on non-obvious topics. Early sign of a reversal: #109 (Apr 21) hit 41K in 24 hours with 7.8% CTR — the first 🟢 breakout in two months.

Five working formulas (recipes that landed)

F1 — Apple + Big paradigm + Author angle

A design system (Liquid Glass, Skeuomorphism, Flat Design) framed by the host’s thesis. Median 118K, n=4.

Why we keep coming back · 206K Reflection / iOS 26 · 146K Welcome back, Skeu · 91K

F2 — Apple + Visual technique + Origin story

A specific Apple technique (radius, gradient, blur) with how it became canon. Median 114K, n=4 — all four landed.

Twenty Pixels · 301K Leopard Dock · 134K Decade of Gradients · 95K

F3 — Apple + Specific product + Thumbnail text

A concrete iPhone/iPad/Mac with a thesis and a text-led thumbnail. Median 95K.

Death of Premium · 128K Most Irrational iPhone · 62K

F4 — Apple + Physical product detail

Edge radius, bezel gap, key shape. Concrete and pointable. Median 65K, n=3 — no fails.

Flat Edge Dilemma · 89K Apple’s 0.5mm · 60K Apple Button · 62K

F5 — UI element told as Story, not History

Cursor, dock, notification, badge — only works as “here’s how it works now”. As History (“here’s where it came from”), the same object loses 9× on median. The genre choice is the variable.

Story · One Feature Refuse to Steal · 173K Story · Icon Violates Rule · 99K History · Door Latch · 13K History · Links Are Blue · 8K
Pre-publish checklist (hard rules)
  • Is there an Apple anchor in the topic or title?
  • Is there text as composition on the thumbnail (not a small caption)?
  • If the title starts with Why/How/What/Does — is the word Apple/iPhone/iPad/macOS/Steve Jobs inside?
  • Is the thumbnail free of physical-Object categories (scroll, car, eyes, charts-as-object, box)?
  • If the topic is a UI element, is the genre Story rather than History or Debunk?
  • If the topic is an abstract Concept, is there a concrete anchor (Gadget, Brand, named artefact)?

Sources & methodology

This wiki is generated from a structured Obsidian vault that mirrors three zones: raw/ (immutable analytics, comms, contracts), wiki/ (curated pages), projects/ (active scripts).

Data

  • Analytics: YouTube Studio Advanced Mode CSV exports. Latest: 2026-04-20 (covers through 2026-04-18).
  • Sample size: n=44 long-form post-#89 videos for benchmarks; n=38 (model) / n=43 (title revision) for the packaging framework.
  • Cutoff rule: all benchmarks exclude videos before episode #89 (2025-03-25), the channel’s growth inflection.
  • Shorts: excluded from benchmarks by policy decision (2026-04-17).

Caveats

  • Classification is subjective. A video can plausibly fit several topic categories. Numbers should be read as tendencies, not laws.
  • Apple is everywhere. 30 of 38 modelled videos. Pure category effects are entangled with the Apple multiplier until non-Apple n grows.
  • Outliers exist. “Hidden Cost of Clean Design” (501K) is unmodelled — the framework predicts 78K. There’s a signal beyond the captured five features.
  • Refresh cadence. Recompute every ~10 new videos.
