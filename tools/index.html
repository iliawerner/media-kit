Tools

Browser-based utilities built for the Design Lovers workflow. No installs, no accounts — open and use.

01
Kinetic Title WebGL

Animated text generator with a WebGL heat-map blur effect. Letters snap into place with kinetic physics — shear, scale, and easing all controllable in real time.

Type your text → adjust SNAP speed and easing sharpness → watch the animation. Move the cursor to shift the heat-map glow.

→ 02
Teleprompter iPad

Converts a script into a mirrored teleprompter link. Designed for the iPad-on-tripod setup: paste text, get a short URL, open it on your iPad through Sidecar.

Paste script → Generate link → Open on iPad via Sidecar → Scroll with MacBook trackpad while recording.

→ 03
Teleprompter 2 Auto-scroll

Auto-scroll version of the teleprompter. Speed is controlled by a joystick or trackpad — the text moves on its own so you don't need to scroll manually while speaking.

Paste script → Set scroll speed → Start auto-scroll → Adjust pace with joystick or trackpad during the take.

→ 04
Viral Predictor Analytics

Estimates view potential of a YouTube video idea using a regression model trained on Design Lovers data. Scores four factors — Recognizability, Relevance, Polarity, and Timeliness — and outputs a predicted view range.

Rate your topic on four sliders → Get a predicted view estimate with confidence interval (model explains ~36% of view variance).
