Teleprompter 2

Auto-scroll mode — joystick or trackpad controls speed, not position.

How to use

  1. Paste your script and click Generate.
  2. Open the link on your Mac in Safari, drag to iPad via Sidecar.
  3. Press any button on your Bluetooth joystick to activate it.
  4. Push stick forward to accelerate, pull back to slow down or reverse.
  5. Release stick — scroll continues at current speed (cruise control).
