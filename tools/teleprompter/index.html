Teleprompter

Paste your script — get a short link with mirrored text, ready for your iPad prompter setup.

How to use

  1. Paste your script and click Generate.
  2. Copy the short link and open it in Safari on your Mac.
  3. Connect your iPad as an external display via Sidecar.
  4. Drag the browser window to the iPad screen.
  5. Scroll with your MacBook trackpad while reading — the mirrored text is readable through the teleprompter glass.
